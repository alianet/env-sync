# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project. Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas and code rather than people, and accept constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [m.kaczanowski@alianet.pl](mailto:m.kaczanowski@alianet.pl). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other content that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Maintainers will consider the context and impact of an incident when deciding on a response. Anyone receiving a report who has a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from handling it.
